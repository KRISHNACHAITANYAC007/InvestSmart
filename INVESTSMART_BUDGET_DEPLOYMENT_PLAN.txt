6. BACKUPS, MAIL, MONITORING AND FREE ALTERNATIVES
==============================================
Backups: Cloudflare R2 STANDARD private bucket. Free allowance is 10 GB-month,
1 million Class A and 10 million Class B operations/month; internet egress free.
Beyond free storage, USD 0.015/GB-month, plus operations [S7]. Require account/
billing approval; alerts are not necessarily hard caps. No public bucket/CDN.
Encrypt before upload, scope write credentials, separately safeguard decryption
and application encryption keys. Database + matching migration/release metadata
must be restorable. Same-VM snapshots alone do not satisfy off-host recovery.

Proposed retention for approval: 7 daily + 4 weekly + 3 monthly encrypted snapshots.
Fourteen 100 MB copies approximate 1.4 GB; fourteen 1 GB copies approximate 14 GB.
Actual compression, versions, WAL/binlogs, retained releases and overlaps affect
storage. Measure before promising free retention. Rs 50 is an allowance, not a
backup-size forecast. Never shorten essential retention merely to stay free.
Propose initial RPO <=24h and measured restore target <=4h for student scale;
these are NOT verified commitments. If losing up to a day of virtual history is
unacceptable, approve more frequent consistent backups/binlog recovery and retest.
Use a consistent logical dump/appropriate supported backup, not an arbitrary copy
of live MySQL files. Verify restore, ledger/factor reconciliation and coordinated
rollback with writes stopped in an isolated environment before DATA-02 passes.

SMTP: Brevo Free, 300 sends/day shared across all approved account traffic [S8].
Use authenticated relay smtp-relay.brevo.com on allowed STARTTLS 587 (or approved
2525); keep mandatory TLS and hostname verification. Sender/DKIM/DMARC and actual
receipt/HTTPS links must pass. Do not run your own mail server to avoid payment.
Mail quota is a capacity limit: verification, resets and pairing all consume it.
Do not bypass verification when quota expires; review upgrade cost before launch.

Monitoring: Better Stack Free offers 10 monitors/heartbeats and email alerts [S9].
Use health, backup/job heartbeat and sanitized status information. Add low-overhead
bounded probes for CPU, container RSS/limits, JVM heap/nonheap/GC, DB wait/pool,
disk growth and application job/mail/provider health; host resource alerting still
needs configuration and actual delivery acceptance. No session-replay integration
or raw authenticated payload upload is proposed. Uptime alone is insufficient.
On restricted staging, use approved probe egress or outbound heartbeat; do not
open the whole service merely to accommodate a monitoring provider's IP changes.

- Render Free: acceptable preview/static demo route, not this accepted always-on
